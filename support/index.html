Support

Help for the current supported release of Sudokukai.

Supported version

This page covers Sudokukai 1.0.0 for iOS and Android. You can find the installed app version under Settings → About → App version.

Start, pause, and continue a game

  1. On Home, choose an unlocked level and select Start.
  2. During a puzzle, select Pause. Select Continue to return to the board.
  3. Sudokukai saves and pauses the current game when the app leaves the foreground. Return to Home and select Continue to resume it.

Replace a saved game

Sudokukai keeps one current game. To replace it, choose a level on Home, select Start, and confirm the replacement warning. The current saved game will be replaced; cancel the warning if you want to keep it.

Levels and strategies

Sudokukai has six levels, each introducing a strategy to practice:

  • Easy: Naked Single.
  • Medium: Hidden Single.
  • Hard: Naked Pair.
  • Advanced: Hidden Pair.
  • Expert: X-Wing.
  • Master: XY-Wing.

Easy is available immediately. Each later level requires three completed games at every preceding level. For example, Hard remains locked until you complete at least three Easy games and three Medium games. Home shows your progress toward the next level.

Learn a strategy before you play

On Home, choose a level and open Learn beneath the level selector. You will see a visual example on a Sudoku grid and a short explanation of the selected strategy. Use it to get familiar with the pattern before starting a puzzle.

Notes and hints

  • Select Notes to enter or remove candidate digits instead of final answers. If automatic note cleanup is enabled, placing a final digit removes that candidate from related cells.
  • Select Hint to highlight where to look. Then select Answer to reveal the logical step and its explanation. Select Apply when it is offered to apply that step to the board.

Multi-select: edit notes across several cells

Use multi-select to update candidate notes in a group of empty cells without editing each cell separately.

  1. Turn on Notes during a game.
  2. Drag your finger across the empty cells you want to select. Cells with final digits or puzzle clues are skipped.
  3. Lift your finger to keep the group selected. Tap another empty cell to add it, or tap a selected cell to remove it from the group. You can also drag across more cells to extend the selection.
  4. Tap a candidate digit to add it to every selected cell. If all selected cells already contain that note, tapping the digit removes it from the whole group instead.

To clear all notes from the selected cells at once, tap Erase while the group is selected.

Turn off Notes to leave multi-select and return to entering final answers. Multi-select is unavailable while a hint is active.

Read the number pad

The small dots beside each digit show how many instances of that digit remain to be placed. Once a digit is complete, its input is disabled. These indicators also appear beside candidate digits during multi-select.

Track your progress

Open Statistics to see:

  • the number of games completed and games started;
  • your best recorded time and best times by level;
  • hints used across completed games; and
  • the strategies used through hints and how often each was used.

Strategy usage reflects your use of hints, rather than every technique you apply on your own. Statistics are stored locally on your device.

Customize your game

Open Settings to adjust:

  • Theme: choose Light, Dark, or System to follow your device appearance.
  • Language: use System default or choose English, Ukrainian, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Italian, Polish, Dutch, Czech, Turkish, Indonesian, Vietnamese, or Russian.
  • Sound and Haptics: turn each on or off.
  • Show timer: show or hide time values.
  • Highlight same numbers: highlight matching digits when you select a filled cell.
  • Highlight row, column and box: highlight the groups related to your selected cell.
  • Clean notes automatically: remove matching candidate notes from related cells when you place a final digit.

Startup and storage troubleshooting

  • If Sudokukai says that the puzzle database could not be opened, check that the device has available storage and select Retry.
  • If a game does not open or a local operation fails, return to Home and try again. Avoid clearing app data or uninstalling while you are trying to preserve a saved game.
  • On the unexpected-error screen, try Retry first. Restart app state resets temporary in-memory state without intentionally deleting saved games or settings.

Local data

Games, settings, progress, and statistics are stored locally on your device. Sudokukai has no account or cloud synchronization, so support cannot view or restore this data. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove it. Depending on your system settings, the operating system may include local app data in a device backup and restore it.

Report a defect

Please include:

  • the Sudokukai version;
  • iOS or Android version and device model;
  • the steps that reproduce the problem;
  • what you expected and what happened instead; and
  • a screenshot only if it is useful and safe to share.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment-card details, private database files, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
