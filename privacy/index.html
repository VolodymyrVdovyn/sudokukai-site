Mobile app data

Sudokukai does not require an account. The mobile app does not collect, transmit, or share user or device data. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not use data to track users.

Gameplay data, settings, current and completed games, puzzle progress, and statistics are stored only on your device in a local database. This may include entries, notes, moves, mistakes, hints, difficulty, timestamps, elapsed time, streaks, and completion statistics. You can remove this data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Depending on your Apple or Android system settings, local app data may be included in a system backup and restored by the platform.

Crash diagnostics

Sudokukai does not include an application-level crash-reporting service and does not send errors, diagnostics, screenshots, session replays, performance traces, or advertising identifiers to the developer or a crash-reporting provider. Error recovery remains on the device.

Apple and Google may generate standard system crash reports according to your device and store settings. This platform functionality is not controlled or extended by Sudokukai and is governed by the applicable platform privacy settings and policies.

Because Sudokukai has no account or server synchronization, the developer cannot access or restore your local gameplay data.

Support email

If you voluntarily contact support by email, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is received through an external email service; it is not collected or transmitted by the Sudokukai mobile app. It is used only to respond to and resolve your request, troubleshoot the reported issue, and protect the developer's legal rights. The email provider and network infrastructure may process the message to deliver it. The legal bases are taking steps at your request and the developer's legitimate interests in providing support and protecting legal rights.

Support correspondence is retained for no more than 12 months after the request is resolved, unless longer retention is required by law or is necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. You may ask the developer to delete it using the privacy contact above. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Device features

The app may read your device language to select its interface language, play bundled sounds, and use device haptics when enabled. These actions happen on the device. Sudokukai does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, health data, or advertising identifiers.

Website and external services

The Settings screen contains fixed links to this Privacy Policy and the Support page. The operating system opens them in your browser. This website stores a light-or-dark theme preference in your browser's local storage; it does not use cookies or submit that preference to the developer. GitHub Pages, email providers, and network operators may process standard connection or message-delivery data under their own policies and may operate outside your country. This browser and email processing is separate from the app's local gameplay data and does not change the App Store declaration that the app itself collects no data.

Children

Sudokukai is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children. It does not knowingly request personal information from children or other users. If a child sends personal information in a support message, contact the developer to request its removal.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or objection to the processing of your support correspondence, and you may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Use the privacy contact above to make a request.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated before the relevant version is released. Privacy requests are supported in English and Ukrainian, and the developer aims to respond within five business days.
