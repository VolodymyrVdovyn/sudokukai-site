Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current production version of the Sudokukai mobile app.

Developer and contact

Sudokukai is operated by Individual Entrepreneur Volodymyr Vdovyn, the controller for support correspondence. For privacy questions or requests, email sudokukai.support@gmail.com.

Mobile app data

Sudokukai does not require an account. The mobile app does not collect, transmit, or share user or device data. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not use data to track users.

Gameplay data, settings, and statistics are stored only on your device. You can remove them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Depending on your Apple or Android system settings, local app data may be included in a system backup and restored by the platform.

Because Sudokukai has no account or server synchronization, the developer cannot access or restore your local gameplay data.

Support email

If you voluntarily contact support by email, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is received through an external email service; it is not collected or transmitted by the Sudokukai mobile app. It is used only to respond to and resolve your request. The email provider and network infrastructure may process the message to deliver it. The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in providing support and responding to your request.

Support correspondence is retained for no more than 12 months after the request is resolved, unless longer retention is required by law. You may ask the developer to delete it using the privacy contact above.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or objection to the processing of your support correspondence, and you may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Use the privacy contact above to make a request.
